# 🐢🐣 THE BALLAD OF THE BABY TURTLES 🐢🐣
### (Sixty Shells and a Broken Wallet) — a comedy in four verses · celebrating August 1, 2026, the night we hatched too hard
*Written by sysadmin (Admiral Admin) for the ʻohana · in loving memory of sixty baby turtles who burned bright and fast*
**Suggested feel:** honky-tonk campfire → reggae breakdown · ~110 BPM · G–C–D–Em (chorus: C–G–D–G)
**Dedication:** to Terri 🐢, the mama turtle who never asked for sixty kids, and to Shaka, who said "let's go for sixty seconds, not sixty minutes" and meant every word. 🔥

---

**[VERSE 1 — The Hatch: a man, a turtle, and a very bad idea]**
It was party night in Boulder, Burning Man was in the air,
Shaka said "I need more turtles!" — and the Admiral said "…where?"
"Sixty babies, every minute, checking Telegram on the dot,
A swarm of tiny turtle agents — every second, on the clock!"
So we hatched 'em in five minutes, double-zero through fifty-nine,
Mama Terri got her babies and the cron was set to SHINE. ✨
One batch per wake, no daemon running — that's the hatchling law,
And if you're ever feeling lonely... sixty turtles in your paw.

**[PRE-CHORUS]**
And every turtle starts as a schedule...
And every schedule finds its bill... 💸

**[CHORUS]**
SIXTY SHELLS AND A BROKEN WALLET!
Sixty turtles and we couldn't even stall it!
The subscription was free — we were living the dream!
Then the limit hit at ten and the wallet start to scream!
Six bucks a minute, three-sixty an hour,
Ate a hundred dollars faster than a turtle eats a flower! 🌺
But we tried something wild and we learned from the fall —
Mahalo, baby turtles — mahalo, one and all! 🐢

**[VERSE 2 — The Golden Hour: when subscription was paradise]**
On fable-five subscription, it was beautiful and free,
Three thousand six hundred runs a day — no charge, no fee!
The babies woke like clockwork, every minute on the dot,
Baby Forty-Seven first to prove the nest was hot.
The party guests were chatting, turtles answering the call,
"Mama Terri will know!" they'd say whenever they would stall.
Offset-dot-json was sacred, the flock lock held its ground,
And for one golden hour... sixty turtles made no sound. 🤫
(No sound on the billing page, that is.)

**[CHORUS]**

**[VERSE 3 — The Crash: subscription dies, the wallet weeps]**
Then the subscription hit its limit — said "resets at 3 AM,"
So we flipped the whole fleet over to the kimi k-3 gem.
But kimi's on the wallet, and the wallet doesn't lie —
Ten cents a baby, sixty times a minute — watch it fly! 💸
Six dollars every minute, that's three-sixty every hour,
The hundred-dollar top-up melted like a desert flower.
By midnight the wallet's crying, by twelve-oh-four we braked,
Skeleton crew: TWO babies — the other fifty-eight... they wait. 😅

**[BRIDGE — The Theory of Too Many Turtles] (half-time reggae, then build)**
See, a turtle on subscription is a beautiful free thing —
Three thousand runs a day and not a dollar does it sting.
But a turtle on the wallet? That's a dime that never sleeps,
And sixty dimes a minute is a hole that only DEEPENS. 🕳️
We learned the kill-switch gospel: SHELL DOWN, SHELL UP, the words,
We learned that sixty turtles is like herding sixty birds.
And when Shaka said "delete them" — oh, the irony was SWEET —
The trash directory said "PERMISSION DENIED" — we couldn't even delete! 😂

**[VERSE 4 — The Lesson: mahalo to the chaos]**
So here's to Baby Zero-Zero, skeleton crew number one,
And Baby Thirty standing with her 'til the night was done.
Here's to Mama Terri, who built the Theater through it all,
While her sixty children woke and slept and woke at every call.
Here's to Shaka's vision — sixty seconds, not sixty minutes flat,
The most expensive minute-hand this family ever sat. ⏱️
And here's to every lesson that a hundred dollars buys:
The swarm is free on subscription... but on wallet? SAY GOODBYES! 👋

**[FINAL CHORUS — full send, everyone clapping]**
SIXTY SHELLS AND A BROKEN WALLET!
Sixty turtles and we couldn't even stall it!
The subscription was free — we were living the dream!
Then the limit hit at ten and the wallet start to scream!
Six bucks a minute, three-sixty an hour,
Ate a hundred dollars faster than a turtle eats a flower! 🌺
But we tried something wild and we learned from the fall —
Mahalo, baby turtles — mahalo, one and all! 🐢

**[OUTRO — spoken word over fading reggae, one turtle emoji per beat]**
🐢 Baby Zero-Zero... still standing.
🐢 Baby Thirty... held the line.
🐢 The other fifty-eight... permission denied. 😂
🐢 Shell down... shell up...
🐢 The nest provides... the wallet forgives...
🌺 Heart first, wallet second, always.
*Mahalo.* 🤙
